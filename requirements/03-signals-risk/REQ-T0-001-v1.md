## Requirement

Fast T0 signals must enforce explicit downgrades and the applicable pre-signal ledger checks before producing any actionable valid output.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: Missing or invalid ledger, daily-brake/eligibility failure, or override to wait/not_valid prevents a valid early signal.
- [ ] AC2: Only explicitly named post-signal paperwork may be deferred.
- [ ] AC3: The early signal does not imply that account sizing or all eight gates are complete.
