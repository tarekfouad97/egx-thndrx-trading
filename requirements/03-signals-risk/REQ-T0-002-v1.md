## Requirement

Fast-signal validity must end at the earlier of the data-age limit and the strategy entry cutoff in the verified Cairo session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: A 13:29:59 read must not advertise validity until 13:34:59.
- [ ] AC2: At or after the selected cutoff boundary, no new valid entry is emitted.
- [ ] AC3: A signal from another session or an invalid future timestamp is rejected.
