## F1 — High: fast signals omit workflow and override enforcement

[`t0_signal`](../../skills/egx-check/scripts/egx_gates.py) calls `t0_verdict` directly. [`cmd_t0_signal`](../../skills/egx-check/scripts/egx.py) does not supply the ledger context or apply the downgrade-only overrides used by full computation. It prints an actionable verdict with entry, stop and target levels, and the skill asks Claude to relay the signal immediately. Consequently, a signal can pass its chart/news gates while missing the full path's prerequisite evidence or an explicit downgrade.

The fast path legitimately defers screenshot/report work, but should define and enforce the remaining prerequisite set before displaying an actionable result. Add regression cases for missing pre-signal ledger evidence and a wait override. Requirement: [REQ-T0-001-v1](../../requirements/03-signals-risk/REQ-T0-001-v1.md).

## F2 — High: signal validity can extend beyond the entry cutoff

In [`egx_gates.py`](../../skills/egx-check/scripts/egx_gates.py), expiry is always analysis-receipt time plus 300 seconds; freshness checks age but do not clamp validity to the session entry cutoff. The underlying verdict checks the recorded observation window rather than the current clock.

Clock-isolation reproduction: stub the underlying verdict as valid, use a Cairo receipt at 2026-11-11 13:29:59 and evaluate 61 seconds later. The wrapper retains valid and an expiry of 13:34:59, although the configured entry window has ended. This isolates the wrapper defect; it is not a live-market end-to-end reproduction. Validate the current session window and clamp expiry. Requirement: [REQ-T0-002-v1](../../requirements/03-signals-risk/REQ-T0-002-v1.md).

## F3 — High: missing partial-exit history is treated as “not done”

[`egx_manage.py`](../../skills/egx-check/scripts/egx_manage.py) omits `partial_done` from required facts and branches on `not h.get("partial_done")`. An unknown history can therefore trigger another partial exit or the pre-partial time stop.

Reproduction: price 32.1, average 30, quantity 101, stop and first stop 28, entry date 2026-11-08, ATR 1 and a post-entry candle `["2026-11-09",30,33,29,32]`, with no `partial_done`. Management returns partial, sell 50, and an empty missing list. Require explicit boolean state for history-dependent decisions while preserving independently justified emergency exits. Requirement: [REQ-POS-001-v1](../../requirements/04-position-management/REQ-POS-001-v1.md).

## F4 — Medium: partial exits can have no executable quantity

The same management fixture with quantity 1 returns partial with “sell 0 of 1” and `sell_shares=None`. Numeric parsing also does not establish a positive whole-share invariant. Validate quantities and define an explicit policy for positions too small to split. Test one share, odd quantities, fractional quantities and negatives. Requirement: [REQ-POS-002-v1](../../requirements/04-position-management/REQ-POS-002-v1.md).

The report already renders the partial action as “Sell half”; that label is not a missing feature. The defect is in the state/quantity contract feeding it.

## F5 — High: replay execution does not match the live plan contract

[`tests/backtest.js`](../../tests/backtest.js), particularly `simulate`, permits half-share exits through a 0.5 fraction, including one-share positions. Pullback entries can fill at a next open above the planned limit up to `maxEntry`. Daily OHLC alone cannot establish whether a target occurred before or after an intraday limit entry; the implementation does not conservatively resolve those sequences. A stop raised after the partial is not checked again against the same bar. Historical analysis also consumes the growing prefix rather than consistently matching the live exported window. Fees are duplicated, and management replay does not fully reproduce live trailing behavior.

Define executable fill/quantity rules, a conservative ambiguous-bar policy, the analysis-window contract and shared cost/rule inputs before treating output as representative of live execution. Requirement: [REQ-BT-002-v1](../../requirements/05-research-backtesting/REQ-BT-002-v1.md).

## F6 — Medium: backtest “pass” and drawdown labels overstate the evidence

The replay pools trades and calculates a normal interval using `1.96 * sd / sqrt(n)`, without accounting for overlapping trades or shared market exposure. Its drawdown is based on cumulative realized R ordered by exits, not marked-to-market account equity with concurrent capital constraints. A threshold-based `pass` does not establish a tradable edge.

Label these outputs exploratory and distinguish trade-series drawdown from portfolio drawdown. Document dependence assumptions, sample selection and out-of-sample evaluation before adding stronger claims. Requirement: [REQ-BT-002-v1](../../requirements/05-research-backtesting/REQ-BT-002-v1.md).
